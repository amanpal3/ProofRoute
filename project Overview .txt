  ## 🎯 What Are We Doing on This Project?

  ProofRoute is a hybrid Web3 + AI Product Provenance, Document Integrity, and Risk Analysis   
  Platform designed for international trade supply chains.

  │ Core Value Proposition:
  │ "Verify where a product came from, what happened during shipment, and whether its
  │ documents were changed."

       [Manufacturer] ──▶ Connects Wallet & Registers Product
              │
              ▼
       [Certificate Upload] ──▶ SHA-256 Hash Anchored On-Chain (ProofRouteRegistry.sol)        
              │
              ▼
       [Logistics Operator] ──▶ Updates Milestones: CREATED ➔ IN_TRANSIT ➔ DELIVERED
              │
              ▼
       [QR Code Generated]
              │
              ▼
       [Customer / Auditor] ──▶ Scans QR Code (No Wallet Required)
              │
              ├── 1. Product Identity & Origin Check
              ├── 2. Shipment History & Journey Timeline
              ├── 3. Cryptographic Document Integrity (VALID vs TAMPERED)
              └── 4. Explainable AI Risk Score & Reasoning Breakdown
